The website for PT. Bintang Surya Teknik Persada, an industrial equipment distributor in South Tangerang, Indonesia. It showcases our electric motors, gearboxes, pumps, and inverters, and lets customers request a quote over WhatsApp.

[![Astro](https://img.shields.io/badge/Astro-4.x-BC52EE?logo=astro&logoColor=white)](https://astro.build)
[![Tailwind CSS](https://img.shields.io/badge/Tailwind_CSS-3.x-06B6D4?logo=tailwindcss&logoColor=white)](https://tailwindcss.com)
[![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-Proprietary-red)](./LICENSE)

## Built with

Astro, Tailwind CSS, and plain JavaScript.

## Updating content

- Company details (contact info, address): `src/data/siteInfo.ts`
- Products: `PRODUCT_DATA` in `public/js/app.js`
